CLIENT PROJECT

stock & dispatch

Stock and dispatch app for a growing business. Staff log in, track stock, dispatch orders and see the money per order.

TYPE
business app
ROLE
Solo: requirements to deploy
STACK
pwa · serverless api · sql
STATUS
Installed by the client, in testing

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Stock and production logged per site, with short and low stock flagged
  2. Today’s orders, split into to load and done
  3. Loading checklist, item by item, until the order is ready for billing
  4. Bill made from what was actually loaded
  5. Payment recorded: cash, UPI or cheque, full or part
  6. Receipt sent on WhatsApp or SMS, money totals update

what i built

  • Staff logins

    Each person signs in with their own PIN. A forgotten PIN resets by email.

  • Fingerprint login

    Unlock with the phone fingerprint instead of typing a PIN.

  • Works offline

    Entries are kept on the phone and sync to the server when it is back online.

  • Money per order

    See what each order is worth and what is still due.

  • Stock and dispatch

    Stock and outgoing orders tracked in one place instead of a register.

  • Built in phases

    Requirements approved first, then six build phases, each one reviewed.
