what i built

  • Works where you post

    LinkedIn, X, Threads, Reddit and Gmail, from the Chrome side panel.

  • Your voice

    Profiles and editable tones so drafts sound like you, not a bot.

  • Private by design

    Bring your own API key. Nothing is sent to a server of mine.

  • 17 languages

    Drafts in the language of the conversation.

  • Daily goals

    Set how many replies a day and track it.

  • Prompt-injection gate

    Posts that try to hijack the AI are caught before drafting.
